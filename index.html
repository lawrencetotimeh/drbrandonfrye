About

Nine years in DC classrooms and school leadership

Dr. Frye began as a middle school mathematics teacher at Friendship Public Charter Schools. He moved to Paul Laurence Dunbar High School, where he has served as Math Department Chair and honors Algebra 1 and Geometry teacher, Interim 11th Grade Assistant Principal, and School Redesign Instructional Coach. He now directs the school's Saturday School program, which he designed and built from the ground up.

His coaching work centers on task design and cognitive demand, and he facilitates professional learning communities focused on rigor, critical literacy, and student-centered pedagogy.

He is a New Leaders Principal Fellow and a DCPS Aspiring Assistant Principal Fellow, and served as Assistant Principal for Washington DC's Summer Learning Academy.
